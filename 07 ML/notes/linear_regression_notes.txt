Linear Regression - Full Explanation

Model:
y = mx + c

Where:
m = slope
c = intercept

----------------------------------------
Given Data:
x = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
y = [2, 4, 6, 8, 10]

----------------------------------------
Table:
x | y | x^2 | xy
1 | 2 | 1   | 2
2 | 4 | 4   | 8
3 | 6 | 9   | 18
4 | 8 | 16  | 32
5 | 10| 25  | 50

Sum:
Σx = 15
Σy = 30
Σx^2 = 55
Σxy = 110
n = 5

----------------------------------------
Slope (m):
m = (nΣxy - ΣxΣy) / (nΣx^2 - (Σx)^2)

m = (5*110 - 15*30) / (5*55 - 15^2)
m = (550 - 450) / (275 - 225)
m = 100 / 50
m = 2

----------------------------------------
Intercept (c):
c = ȳ - m x̄

x̄ = 15/5 = 3
ȳ = 30/5 = 6

c = 6 - (2*3)
c = 0

----------------------------------------
Final Equation:
y = 2x

----------------------------------------
Prediction Example:
If x = 6:
y = 2*6 = 12

----------------------------------------
Conclusion:
This is a perfect linear relationship where all points lie on a straight line.

m = 50 / 45

Step 3: Intercept (c)

ȳ = 20/5 = 4

c = ȳ - m x̄
c = 4 - (0.9 * 3)
c = 4 - 2.7 = 1.3
Final Equation:
y = 0.9x + 1.3
Meaning:
For every 1 unit increase in x, y increases by approximately 0.9

============================== PYTHON BITWISE OPERATORS NOTES
==============================

1) Binary Basics
Right shift means divide by 2.
Left shift means multiply by 2.

Binary numbers use only 0 and 1.

Place values (from right): 2^0 = 1 2^1 = 2 2^2 = 4 2^3 = 8 2^4 = 16

Example: 100 (binary) = 1×4 + 0×2 + 0×1 = 4 110 (binary) = 1×4 + 1×2 +
0×1 = 6 1100 (binary) = 8 + 4 = 12

2) Right Shift (>>)

Rule: a >> n = a / (2^n)

Example: 8 >> 1 = 4 8 >> 2 = 2 8 >> 3 = 1

Right shift means divide by 2.

3) Left Shift (<<)

Rule: a << n = a × (2^n)

Example: 4 << 1 = 8 4 << 2 = 16 4 << 3 = 32

Left shift means multiply by 2.

4) Bitwise AND (&)

Rule: 1 & 1 = 1 Otherwise = 0

Example: 12 = 1100 10 = 1010

1100 1010 —- 1000 = 8

5) Bitwise OR (|)

Rule: If any one bit is 1 → result 1

1100 1010 —- 1110 = 14

6) Bitwise XOR (^)

Rule: Different bits → 1 Same bits → 0

1100 1010 —- 0110 = 6

7) Bitwise NOT (~)

Rule in Python: ~n = -(n + 1)

Example: ~12 = -13 ~10 = -11

SHORT SUMMARY

    Divide by 2 << Multiply by 2 & Both 1 হলে 1 | Any 1 হলে 1 ^
    Different হলে 1 ~ -(n+1)
